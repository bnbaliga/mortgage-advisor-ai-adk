1. Executive Summary

1.1 Business Opportunity

The mortgage industry faces a critical efficiency challenge. Traditional loan origination costs average $12,000 per loan, with 40% attributed to customer acquisition and initial screening. Borrowers experience:

  • Average 3-day wait for loan officer callbacks
  • Limited availability (business hours only)
  • Inconsistent information across representatives
  • High abandonment rates (35%) during initial inquiry phase

1.2 Proposed Solution

HomeFirst AI Mortgage Advisor is a 24/7 voice AI system that:

  1. Answers calls instantly with natural conversation
  2. Screens eligibility against state-specific lending rules
  3. Provides rate estimates with required compliance disclaimers
  4. Captures qualified leads for loan officer follow-up
  5. Services existing borrowers with account information and payments
