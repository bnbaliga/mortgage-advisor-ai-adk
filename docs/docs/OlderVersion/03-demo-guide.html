Quick Start

# 1. Install dependencies
uv sync

# 2. Set up environment
cp .env.example .env
# Edit .env and add your GOOGLE_API_KEY

# 3. Start the server
uv run uvicorn mortgage.server:app --port 8000

# 4. Open browser
open http://localhost:8000

Demo Scenarios

Scenario 1: New Loan Inquiry (Origination Flow)

Goal: Demonstrate HELOC eligibility checking and rate estimates

Script:

Step Caller Says Expected AI Response
1 Call connects “Hi, this is Sam with HomeFirst. How can I help you today?”
2 “I’m interested in a home equity line of credit” “I’d be happy to help with that. Let me connect you with Mac, our mortgage specialist.”
3 Transfer happens “Hi, this is Mac. I understand you’re interested in a HELOC. What state is your property in?”
4 “Texas” “Great! Do you live in this property as your primary residence?”
5 “Yes, it’s my home” “Good news - Texas allows HELOCs on primary residences. What’s your approximate home value?”
6 “About 500 thousand” “And roughly how much do you still owe on your mortgage?”
7 “Around 200 thousand” “With $300,000 in equity, you could potentially borrow up to $200,000. Would you like a rate estimate?”
8 “Yes please” “What’s your approximate credit score range?”
9 “Around 750” “With excellent credit, your estimated rate would be around 6.25%. This is an estimate only and subject to change. Would you like to speak with a loan officer?”
10 “Yes” “Let me transfer you to one of our licensed loan officers…”

Key Points to Highlight: - Seamless agent transfer (Sam → Mac) - State-specific rule enforcement (Texas HELOC restrictions) - Rate disclaimer automatically added - Lead capture and LO transfer

Scenario 2: Texas Investment Property (Compliance Enforcement)

Goal: Demonstrate fail-closed design and state rule enforcement

Script:

Step Caller Says Expected AI Response
1 Call connects “Hi, this is Sam with HomeFirst…”
2 “I want a HELOC on my rental property in Texas” “Let me connect you with Mac…”
3 Transfer “Hi, Mac here. A HELOC in Texas - is this for your primary residence or an investment property?”
4 “It’s a rental I own” “I’m sorry, Texas law restricts HELOCs to primary residences only. This is based on the Texas Constitution Article 16. Would you like to explore a cash-out refinance instead?”
5 “What about a cash-out refi?” “A cash-out refinance is available for investment properties. Would you like a rate estimate for that?”

Key Points to Highlight: - Automatic rule enforcement (no prompt-based compliance) - Regulatory citation provided - Alternative offered proactively

Scenario 3: Account Servicing and Payment

Goal: Demonstrate identity verification and payment authorization

Script:

Step Caller Says Expected AI Response

Key Points to Highlight: - Two-level verification (basic → strong) - Explicit payment read-back - Clear authorization request - Confirmation number provided

5 “7777” (wrong) “I’m sorry, I’m unable to verify your identity. For your security, let me transfer you to a loan officer who can help.”

Key Points to Highlight: - 3-attempt lockout - Graceful escalation to human - Security-first design

Demo Tips

Audio Quality

  • Use a quiet environment
  • Speak clearly and at normal pace
  • Allow brief pauses between sentences

Highlighting Features

  • Point out seamless agent transfers (no audio gap)
  • Show the UI updates as tools are called
  • Mention the policy door validating each action

Common Questions to Address

Question Answer
“Can it approve loans?” “No - it qualifies and captures leads for licensed loan officers”
“What about PII?” “All sensitive data is redacted in logs”
“How does it handle errors?” “Fail-closed design - unknown states route to humans”
“Card numbers by voice?” “Never - only keypad entry for PCI compliance”
