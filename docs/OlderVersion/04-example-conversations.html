     for investment properties in Texas, or you could consider a DSCR

CALLER: What about the cash-out refinance?

SYSTEM: get_mortgage_products(product_type="cashout_refi")

     larger loan, giving you the difference in cash. For investment
     properties, you can typically cash out up to 75% of the property

CALLER: Sure.

MAC: What's the approximate value of the property?

CALLER: About 400 thousand.

MAC: And what do you currently owe on it?

CALLER: 200 thousand.

SYSTEM: calculate_equity(home_value=400000, mortgage_balance=200000)
RESULT: {equity: 200000, max_cashout: 100000}

CALLER: Yes, let me talk to someone.
